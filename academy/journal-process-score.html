Journal & process score

PnL without process is noise. A journal that only tracks money trains ego. Score the standard — then the money has somewhere honest to sit.

Hard line

Pros practice. Tourists collect courses. The journal is the training log. If you will not write what you did, you will not fix what you did.

Process score answers: Did I follow the rules I said I would follow? Body check. Pre-session. Criteria present or stand aside. Size as planned. Stop honoured. Max daily loss respected. Review done.

A green day with a broken process is not a win. A flat day with a clean stand-aside can be.
