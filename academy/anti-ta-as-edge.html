Part 2 · Lesson 1

Why this program is anti-TA-as-edge

Most “edge” online is confluence theatre. We refuse to sell it. Literacy and standards stay. Prediction cosplay goes.

Hard line

Technical analysis as wallpaper is not evil. Treating RSI, MACD, and stacked “confluences” as a proprietary edge is a product model — not a market truth. Indicator packs are wallpaper. Signal rooms fail serious traders. Sim PnL as proof is theatre.

This academy teaches auction and order-flow literacy as questions, not entries. Plays and invalidation sit behind The Floor / strategy gate — process, not calls.

We do not sell signals. We do not sell indicator packs. Process over prediction.

What we keep vs what we refuse

Keep

  • Price, volume, session structure
  • Profile / value as maps
  • Tape, DOM, clusters as questions
  • Risk maths, journal, Constitution
  • ATAS as classroom

Refuse

  • Signals / tip wire / “levels for today”
  • TradingView indicator packs as product
  • Funded-challenge farm promises
  • Fake metrics and P&L flex as proof
  • SOM/MF theatre language

ICT / SMC note (plain)

Some structure language is useful as vocabulary. Marketing layers that turn vocabulary into destiny are not. Keep what helps you describe location. Drop what sells you certainty you did not earn.

Execute (today)

  2. Read the manifesto. If it offends you, good — filter early.
  3. Snap search: “retail theatre”, “no-signals”, “data is king.”
