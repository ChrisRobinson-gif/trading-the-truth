Trading vs gambling vs process

Call it what it is. Most men who “trade” are gambling with a chart open. Process is the line between serious work and retail theatre.

Hard line

Gambling is hope dressed as action. You put money at risk without a defined edge, without a stop that you will honour, without a journal you will face. The outcome is entertainment or denial.

Retail “trading” often looks the same: click when bored, size up after a win, chase after a loss, screenshot the green day, bury the red week. Cosplay.

Process is different. You decide rules before the open. You size from risk, not mood. You stand aside when criteria are absent — and you count that as a win for the standard, not a failure of nerve.

If you will not follow a spend rule, you will not follow a stop. Same muscle. Same room.

Three boxes — put yourself in one

Gambling

  • No written plan; “feel” entries
  • Risk floats with ego
  • No max daily loss that actually stops you
  • Proof = screenshots and stories

Retail theatre

  • Indicators as identity
  • Signal rooms and “levels for today”
  • Sim PnL sold as proof
  • Body ignored; sleep optional

Process

  • Criteria before click
  • R defined; max loss enforced
  • Journal + process score
  • Stand aside is an edge

What we will not do here

We will not sell you hope. We will not send signals. We will not dress gambling as a “system” with more oscillators.

We will teach literacy and standards. Entries and plays sit behind The Floor / strategy gate — as process, not calls.

Execute (today)

  1. Write one sentence: am I gambling, performing, or installing process?
  2. Write your max daily loss in cash terms. If you cannot name it, you are not ready for size.
  3. Open the Snap dictionary. Search “stand aside” and “process.” Read them dry.

Write it down. Then join the training. → The Floor
