Auction Market Theory plain English

Markets discover price by buyers and sellers showing size and aggression. Balance = accepted value. Imbalance = price seeks new value. Maps, not crystal balls.

Hard line

Auction theory is not a secret handshake. It is a plain description of how continuous markets work. Participants advertise willingness. Matching occurs. When two-way trade accepts a region, that region is value for that window. When one side overwhelms and price migrates, the auction is imbalanced — seeking a new place where two-way trade resumes.

Profiles and footprints are maps of that process. They do not owe you an entry. Literacy asks better questions. Plays come later, gated, as criteria — not as a tip wire.

Literacy is not a playbook. Questions, not entries. ATAS is the classroom — not a tip wire.

Words you need (dry)

  • Balance — two-sided trade; accepted prices; often rotational.
  • Imbalance — one-sided pressure; price discovery toward new value.
  • Value area / POC — where the session did business (definitions locked in your journal).
  • Aggression — marketable orders lifting offers / hitting bids.
  • Absorption — aggression that fails to move price; resting size holds.

What this lesson is not

Not Play 31/32/33. Not “buy the VAL.” Not a signal. Public depth stops at literacy. Full Execute play cards = paid strategy / Floor+.

Execute (today)

  1. Define balance vs imbalance in your own words — one sentence each.
  2. On a replay or prior day, mark one balance region and one imbalance migration. No trade required.
  3. Snap: “auction”, “profile”, “CVD”. Read as questions.
